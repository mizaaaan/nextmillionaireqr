What this website stores, what you share with us, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Next Millionaire Co-operative, Al Funduq Street, West Bay, Doha, Qatar. You can reach us at info@nextmillionaireqr.com.

What this website stores

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and visitors do not create accounts. Your browser keeps a few preferences on your own device only: your chosen language, your light or dark theme and, if you unlock the Member Portal, a temporary flag that lasts until you close the tab. This information is never sent to us.

Information you send us

  • Driver registration: when you use the Driver Registration Form (a Google Form) the details you enter are collected by Google on our behalf and shared with us so we can review your application. Google's privacy policy applies to that form.
  • Email and telephone: if you contact us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

Third-party services

To display this website your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and some images and video from third-party content delivery services. Those providers may receive your IP address and device information as part of delivering the files.

How long we keep information

We keep driver applications and correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your request and meet our record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You can clear this site's stored preferences at any time from your browser settings. To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email info@nextmillionaireqr.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
